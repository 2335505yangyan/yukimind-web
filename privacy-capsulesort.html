Capsule Sort Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Capsule Sort is provided by YukiMind ("we", "us", or "our"). If you have questions about this policy or privacy matters, contact us at support@yukimind.com.

1. Local game data

Capsule Sort stores gameplay progress, settings, virtual Acorns, and tool inventory locally on your device. The game does not provide player accounts, cloud saves, social features, or a developer-operated server for gameplay data.

You can remove locally stored game data through Android system app storage controls. Removing local data may permanently remove your progress and inventory from that device.

2. Advertising and consent

The game uses Google Mobile Ads to provide optional rewarded ads and limited interstitial ads. Google’s advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and consent signals for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

Where required, the game uses Google’s consent tools to request and manage advertising choices. You may revisit available advertising privacy choices from the in-game Settings screen.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

Capsule Sort offers optional one-time purchases through Google Play Billing, including virtual Acorn packages and permanent removal of interstitial ads. Payment information is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data handled by Google advertising or Google Play purchase services is processed according to Google’s applicable policies and the selected service configuration.

5. Children

Capsule Sort is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services, or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when changes are published.

7. Contact

For privacy, support, or purchase questions, contact support@yukimind.com.

《Capsule Sort》隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 10 日

《Capsule Sort》由 YukiMind 提供。如你对本政策、隐私或数据处理有疑问，请联系 support@yukimind.com。

1. 本地游戏数据

《Capsule Sort》会在你的设备本地保存游戏进度、设置、虚拟橡果和道具库存。游戏不提供玩家账号、云存档、社交功能或由开发者运营的游戏数据服务器。

你可以通过 Android 系统的应用存储管理功能删除本地游戏数据。删除本地数据可能永久移除该设备上的进度和库存。

2. 广告与同意选择

游戏使用 Google Mobile Ads 提供可选激励广告和有限的插屏广告。Google 广告服务可能会处理设备标识符、由 IP 地址推断的大致位置、应用互动、诊断信息和同意信号，用于广告投放、效果衡量、分析、防欺诈及遵守法律义务。

在适用地区，游戏会使用 Google 的同意工具请求和管理广告选择。你可以在游戏“设置”中重新打开可用的广告隐私选项。

更多信息：Google 隐私权政策。

3. 应用内购买

《Capsule Sort》通过 Google Play Billing 提供可选的一次性购买，包括虚拟橡果包和永久移除插屏广告。付款信息由 Google Play 处理；我们不会接收或保存你的完整银行卡信息。

4. 数据共享

我们不会出售个人信息。Google 广告服务或 Google Play 购买服务处理的数据，将按照 Google 的适用政策及实际服务配置处理。

5. 未成年人

《Capsule Sort》面向 13 岁及以上用户，并非面向儿童。我们不会故意直接收集儿童的个人信息。

6. 政策变更

当游戏、所使用的服务或适用要求发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。发布更新时会同步修改页面顶部的“最后更新”日期。

7. 联系方式

如有隐私、技术支持或购买问题，请联系 support@yukimind.com。
