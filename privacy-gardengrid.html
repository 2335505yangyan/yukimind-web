Garden Grid Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 September 2026

Garden Grid is a logic puzzle game by YukiMind. It does not require an account to play.

Information stored on your device

Garden Grid stores puzzle progress, completed levels, Daily Garden completion, language, audio, haptic and accessibility preferences locally on your device. We do not operate an account database or upload your puzzle progress.

Advertising and consent

Garden Grid uses Google Mobile Ads to provide optional rewarded ads for hints and occasional interstitial ads after completed gardens. Google's SDK may automatically collect or share IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or account identifiers, advertising data, app and ad interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes. Data sent by the SDK is encrypted in transit.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for privacy choices before the advertising SDK is initialized. You can revisit those choices from Garden Grid's Privacy & Support screen when the privacy-options entry point is required. The ads available to you can vary with your region and choices.

Google's handling of this information is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Because Garden Grid has no account system, there is no server account to delete. Advertising data retention and controls are described in Google's policies and your device's advertising and privacy settings.

Children

Garden Grid is not directed to children under the minimum age required in the player's region. We do not knowingly collect children's information through a Garden Grid account because no account exists. Store age targeting and advertising settings must match the released app.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game, advertising SDK or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
