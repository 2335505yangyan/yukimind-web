Fish & Clues Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: November 2, 2026

Fish & Clues (the “game”), Android package com.yukimind.fishclues, is provided by YukiMind (“we”, “us”, or “our”). For privacy questions or requests, email support@yukimind.com.

Summary: Core puzzles work offline, and game progress is stored on your device. The game uses Google Mobile Ads for advertising and Unity In-App Purchasing with Google Play Billing for optional purchases. These providers process data as described below. There is no game account, developer-operated cloud save, social chat, or developer-operated gameplay analytics server.

1. Game data on your device

The game saves level and water progress, puzzle state, tutorial status, fish collections, aquarium selections, daily challenge records and streaks, virtual shells, hint inventory, settings, and purchase entitlements on your device. Local testing or diagnostic records may also be stored; they are not automatically uploaded to a YukiMind server. We receive a record only if you choose to send it to us.

Your operating system or Google account backup settings may back up or restore app data. This is controlled by the platform and is not a Fish & Clues account or a developer-operated cloud save. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game may permanently remove progress and consumable inventory. Restoring an eligible non-consumable purchase does not restore all gameplay progress or spent consumables.

2. Advertising and consent

Google Mobile Ads provides interstitial ads and optional rewarded videos. Its SDK processes IP addresses, which may indicate approximate location, ad and app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account-related identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and App Set ID. Purposes include advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Google states that this SDK encrypts data in transit using TLS.

Where applicable, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform for consent and privacy choices before requesting ads. Available choices can be revisited through the advertising privacy option in Settings. Availability depends on your region and the provider's requirements. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android privacy settings. Declining personalized advertising does not stop every form of processing necessary to deliver or secure ads.

Rewarded videos are optional. A reward is issued when the advertising provider reports that the reward condition has been met. The remove-interstitial-ads purchase stops automatic interstitial ads; it does not remove optional rewarded videos.

Provider information: Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising privacy information.

3. Optional purchases and Unity IAP

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing and Unity In-App Purchasing. Products may include shells, hints or supply bundles, removal of automatic interstitial ads, and access to exhibition content or supported water unlocks. Contents are fixed rather than randomized. Google Play handles payment credentials, billing, and refunds under its rules. We do not receive or store your full payment card or bank details.

The current game uses Unity IAP 5.4.4. When purchase flows run, Unity may process transaction and product records, installation or session identifiers, device and language information, and country inferred from IP addresses, for purchase operation and reporting. Its disclosures also cover diagnostics and performance data. The game does not offer a Unity Webshop login, direct-to-consumer checkout, or a developer-operated receipt-validation server.

See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Platform purchase processing is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Contact and support information

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as a description of an issue, app version, screenshots, or an order reference. We use this to respond, investigate the issue, handle privacy requests, and maintain necessary support records. Do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

5. Recipients and processing purposes

Google and Unity receive information through the services described above. We use service providers for email and public website hosting. Providers may act as processors or as independent controllers depending on the activity. We do not operate an independent advertising mediation network or integrate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics in this release. This does not mean advertising and purchase providers perform no measurement or diagnostics.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising-related disclosures may be treated as “sharing” or a “sale” under some regional laws. You can use the available advertising privacy choices or contact us about rights that apply to you. We may disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, prevent fraud, or secure the services.

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on consent for processing that requires consent, performance of the service you request for purchases and support, legitimate interests in security and service maintenance where permitted, and applicable legal obligations. Providers explain their own processing and legal bases in their policies.

6. Retention and security

Local game data stays on your device until it is cleared, overwritten, removed, or restored through platform controls. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve requests, keep necessary service records, and meet legal obligations. Provider retention periods follow their policies and contractual requirements; some purchase records must remain for legal, security, or accounting reasons.

We use reasonable safeguards and encrypted service connections where supported. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Clearing game data does not automatically delete information already held by advertising or purchase providers.

7. Your choices, access, and deletion requests

  • Clear local game data using Android's app-storage controls. Consider the risk of losing progress and consumable inventory first.
  • Manage available advertising privacy choices in Settings and advertising-ID controls in Android settings.
  • Decline an optional rewarded video or cancel a purchase before confirming it.
  • Request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability where applicable by emailing support@yukimind.com. Describe your request and the app involved; we may ask for limited information to verify it.

There is no Fish & Clues player account to delete. We cannot retrieve a local-only save from our servers. For information processed on our behalf, we will handle or coordinate applicable requests with the relevant provider. Information controlled independently by Google or Unity may also require their own request channels. Requests are subject to legal exceptions and retention duties. Depending on your location, you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Teen and adult audience

Fish & Clues is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults. It is not directed to children under 13. Local laws may require a higher age for independent consent or parental authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review and address the matter. This statement does not mean the game verifies every player's age.

9. International processing and this website

Google, Unity, and other service providers may process information outside your country. Their policies and applicable agreements describe safeguards for international transfers.

This public policy page is hosted through GitHub Pages. The page itself includes no advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts, or tracking cookies. Hosting providers may process IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

10. Policy changes

We may update this policy if the game, providers, or applicable requirements change. We will revise the date above and provide additional notice where required.
