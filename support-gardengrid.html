Garden Grid Support

Garden Grid support is provided by YukiMind.

Before contacting us

  • Use Reset only if you want to clear the current board; completed gardens are not removed.
  • Past Daily Gardens are opened from the Daily calendar. Future dates are unavailable.
  • Music, sound, haptics, language and accessibility options are available in Settings.
  • Rewarded hints and post-completion ads require a network connection. If an ad is unavailable, normal play and navigation remain available.
  • Ad privacy choices appear under Privacy & Support when required for your region.

Report a problem

Email support@yukimind.com with your device model, OS version, Garden Grid version, the garden/date involved, and the first step that failed. Attach a screenshot only if it contains no information you do not wish to share.
